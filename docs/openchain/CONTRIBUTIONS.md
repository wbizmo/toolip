# Open-source contributions policy

**Status: proposed, awaiting accountable approval and participant notification.**

- Inbound external patches: inspect the contributor's authority to submit, origin of copied material, affected copyrights, licensing compatibility, test/security results and conditions attached to files. Preserve external contributors' attribution and rights. A contributor agreement must not be presumed.
- Outbound patches and upstream bug reports: the maintainer documents the destination license and repository, contribution scope, intellectual-property authority, whether the patch includes employer/customer material, dependency/secret exposure and reviewer approval before submission. A patch containing protected data or unidentified third-party code must not be submitted.
- Accept or reject imports/exports only after license and security review, with an archived decision linked to the commit/PR.
- Communicate this policy to all programme participants; retain acknowledgements and examples of completed reviews. Do not mark the ISO/IEC 5230 community-contribution questions complete merely because this file exists.
