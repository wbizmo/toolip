# OpenSSF Best Practices Gold — complete criterion register

Project: Toolip v3.2.1. **Gold status is not yet earned.**

Official criteria: https://www.bestpractices.dev/criteria
Canonical source: https://github.com/ossf/best-practices-badge/blob/main/criteria/criteria.yml

145 level-specific checks: 67 Passing, 55 Silver and 23 Gold. Repeated IDs are intentional.
All MUST items require verified operation; SHOULD items require evidence or a defensible recorded rationale;
SUGGESTED items require consideration. Use N/A only where expressly allowed by the badge form.
Evidence includes an actual URI, version, reviewer and date, not merely an unexecuted policy.

## GitHub reviews: policy versus achieved outcome

Toolip [main branch rules](https://github.com/wbizmo/toolip/rules/21824146)
require two approvals, code-owner review, last-push approval and resolved threads.
Administrator bypass remains possible, so this is **configured-policy evidence only**.
Gold two_person_review requires at least 50% of actual proposed modifications
independently reviewed before release; bypasses without review do not count.
Gold bus_factor and contributors_unassociated require genuine other people.
